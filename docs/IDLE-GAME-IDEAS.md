# Possible persistent idle games

These are proposals for a future addition, not implemented games. Each could
have a per-member, per-server game profile containing buildings, companions,
upgrades, collections and a last-progress timestamp. Returning to the game would
calculate elapsed production and show a summary; it need not send constant
messages or keep a background task alive for every player. Offline earnings
should have a visible, configurable cap and use the bot's clock, not a client
supplied timestamp. Storage survives restarts; collection needs the bot online.

1. **Moonlit Fishing** — hire fishing companions, improve rods and discover rarer catches.
2. **Ghost Sanctuary** — spirits gather keepsakes while you decorate their home.
3. **Midnight Garden** — grow magical flowers, breed colors and unlock new plots.
4. **Velvet Café** — hire staff who serve customers while you expand the menu.
5. **Potion Workshop** — automate ingredient gathering, brewing and fictional shop orders.
6. **Ruins Restoration** — workers clear rubble and rebuild an expanding gothic city.
7. **Crystal Mines** — miners gather gems for deeper shafts and better equipment.
8. **Dragon Nursery** — hatch dragons, build habitats and send adults on expeditions.
9. **Space Colony** — automate mining, research, shipping and planetary expansion.
10. **Enchanted Library** — librarians restore books that unlock rooms and discoveries.
11. **Aquarium Worlds** — fish grow, breed and populate newly unlocked habitats.
12. **Witch Academy** — students study spells and complete passive assignments.
13. **Adventure Guild** — recruit parties and send them on timed, branching quests.
14. **Floating Islands** — build resource chains across collectible sky islands.
15. **Neon Factory** — connect production lines and fulfill increasingly complex orders.
16. **Dream Hotel** — supernatural guests generate fictional income and unlock new wings.
17. **Mushroom Grove** — grow colonies, combine strains and discover glowing varieties.
18. **Starlight Observatory** — telescopes chart the sky and unlock distant research.
19. **Familiar Expeditions** — companions return with materials and collectible discoveries.
20. **Clockwork Workshop** — build helpers who automate increasingly intricate creations.

For long-term progression: achievements, cosmetic milestones, collections,
optional prestige resets and seasonal side areas can add goals without requiring
constant interaction. Any leaderboards should support opting out. These systems
should use fictional resources without real-money payouts.
