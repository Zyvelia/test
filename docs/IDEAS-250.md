# 250 more Vivi feature ideas

Wishlist only: these are proposed additions. Each item now includes a short explanation of what it would do.

## Moderation and safety

1. **Moderation confirmation buttons** — Ask a moderator to confirm a serious action, such as a ban, before Vivi applies it.
2. **Private staff notes** — Let authorized staff attach private notes to a member for future reference.
3. **New-account risk alerts** — Notify staff when someone joins with a recently created Discord account so they can review the new arrival.
4. **Configurable warning expiration** — Make warnings stop counting toward punishment thresholds after a configurable period.
5. **Warning removal with audit reasons** — Let authorized staff remove a warning while recording who removed it and why.
6. **Moderation action undo guidance** — Explain how to reverse supported actions, such as timeouts; deleted messages would not become recoverable.
7. **Staff case assignment** — Assign a moderation case to a specific staff member so responsibility is clear.
8. **Case evidence attachments** — Attach message links, screenshots, or other evidence to a moderation case.
9. **Repeat-offender review queues** — Collect repeated incidents into a review queue so staff can assess the pattern.
10. **Graduated moderation action ladders** — Configure progressively stronger responses to repeated violations, with appropriate staff controls.
11. **Channel-specific moderation policies** — Apply different moderation rules in different channels, such as stricter rules in announcements.
12. **Attachment extension restrictions** — Allow or block specified file types in selected channels.
13. **Oversized attachment filtering** — Remove or flag attachments above a server-defined size limit.
14. **Unicode impersonation alerts** — Flag names that use lookalike characters to resemble another member or staff account.
15. **Suspicious display-name detection** — Flag display names that match configured scam patterns or imitate protected staff names.
16. **Compromised-account report workflows** — Guide members through reporting a possibly compromised account and alert the appropriate staff.
17. **Mass role-change alerts** — Alert administrators when many roles are added or removed in a short period.
18. **Unexpected administrator-role alerts** — Notify trusted administrators when a role gains powerful permissions such as Administrator.
19. **Server settings tamper alerts** — Alert staff when monitored server settings change unexpectedly.
20. **Moderator handover summaries** — Summarize unresolved cases and recent incidents when another moderation shift takes over.
21. **Staff conduct review forms** — Provide a structured way to report concerns about a staff member's behavior.
22. **Member blocklist appeal tracking** — Track requests to review or remove a member from a server-maintained blocklist.
23. **Configurable moderator action limits** — Limit how many moderation actions a staff member can perform within a chosen period.
24. **Emergency staff announcement buttons** — Provide prepared emergency notices that authorized staff can publish quickly.
25. **Permission-overwrite explanation reports** — Explain which role or channel override allows or blocks a particular permission.
